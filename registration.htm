Useful information

When are the scientific sessions?

The scientific programme is planned for 5 December, 09:00–17:00, and 6 December, 09:00–12:30. Arrival and informal networking take place on 4 December. All times are Hong Kong time (UTC+8).

Where will the forum take place?

The forum will be held at City University of Hong Kong. All scientific sessions take place in one room. The specific room and arrival arrangements will be announced.
